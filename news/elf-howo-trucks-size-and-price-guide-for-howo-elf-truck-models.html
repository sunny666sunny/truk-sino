Elf HOWO Trucks: Complete Size and Price Guide for HOWO Elf Models

The HOWO Elf truck series represents SINOTRUK's entry into the light to medium-duty truck segment, designed for urban delivery, distribution, and light construction applications. The Elf series combines the reliability of SINOTRUK engineering with the maneuverability and efficiency required for city operations.

The HOWO Elf truck is available in several configurations including cargo truck, box truck, and refrigerated truck variants. The standard model features a 4x2 drive configuration with a gross vehicle weight (GVW) ranging from 4.5 to 8 tons, making it suitable for operations requiring a standard driver's license in most jurisdictions.

Key dimensions of the HOWO Elf truck include an overall length of approximately 5,995mm, width of 2,050mm, and height of 2,350mm. The cargo body offers a length of 4,200mm, providing ample space for urban delivery applications. The compact dimensions ensure excellent maneuverability in congested city streets and tight loading docks.

The HOWO Elf is powered by a fuel-efficient diesel engine with power output ranging from 120HP to 160HP, depending on the model. The engine is paired with a 6-speed manual transmission, providing smooth power delivery and good fuel economy. The suspension system is tuned for comfort during urban driving while maintaining adequate load-carrying capability.

Pricing for the HOWO Elf truck series is competitive within the light truck segment, offering excellent value for businesses seeking reliable urban delivery vehicles. The combination of SINOTRUK's manufacturing quality, parts availability, and dealer support makes the Elf series an attractive option for fleet operators looking to add light-duty vehicles to their operations.
