HOWO 12 Wheeler Dump Truck Price: Philippines, India & Global Market Guide

The HOWO 12 wheeler dump truck represents the largest standard configuration in the SINOTRUK dump truck lineup, offering maximum payload capacity for heavy-duty mining and large-scale construction applications. This guide provides comprehensive pricing information for the 12 wheeler configuration across key markets.

The HOWO 12 wheeler dump truck typically features an 8x4 drive configuration with twelve wheels total, providing exceptional load distribution and stability. The payload capacity ranges from 30 to 40 tons, with a cargo body volume of 22-30 cubic meters. The vehicle is powered by SINOTRUK's most powerful engines, with ratings from 371HP to 420HP or higher.

In the Philippines, the HOWO 12 wheeler dump truck price ranges from 4.5 million to 5.5 million Philippine Pesos for new vehicles. Second-hand 12 wheeler models, typically 3-5 years old, are available from 2.5 million to 3.5 million Pesos, depending on condition and maintenance history. The 12 wheeler configuration is preferred for large-scale mining operations and major infrastructure projects.

In India, the 12 wheeler dumper price varies by manufacturer, with HOWO competing in the heavy-duty segment. The 12 wheeler dumper price in India ranges from approximately 35 lakhs to 50 lakhs INR for comparable specifications. The Indian market has specific requirements for vehicle dimensions and specifications that may differ from other markets.

The 12 wheeler configuration offers significant advantages for high-volume material transport. The additional axles and wheels provide better weight distribution, reducing ground pressure and improving flotation on soft surfaces. The increased payload capacity improves productivity by reducing the number of trips required for a given volume of material. For operations requiring maximum productivity, the HOWO 12 wheeler dump truck is an excellent investment.
