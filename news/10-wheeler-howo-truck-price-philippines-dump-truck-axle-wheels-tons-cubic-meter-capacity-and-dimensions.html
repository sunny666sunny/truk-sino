10 Wheeler HOWO Truck Price Philippines: Axle, Wheels, Tons & Capacity Guide

The 10 wheeler HOWO truck is one of the most sought-after heavy-duty vehicle configurations in the Philippine market, renowned for its exceptional payload capacity and durability in construction and mining applications. The 10 wheeler configuration typically features a 6x4 drive system with ten wheels total, providing superior stability and load distribution.

The HOWO 10 wheeler dump truck offers a payload capacity of 20-25 tons, with a cargo body volume ranging from 16 to 22 cubic meters depending on the specific configuration. The standard dimensions include an overall length of approximately 8,500mm, width of 2,500mm, and height of 3,450mm. The wheelbase measures around 3,800mm + 1,350mm for the tandem axle configuration.

Powering the 10 wheeler HOWO truck is SINOTRUK's WD615 series engine, available in 336HP, 371HP, and 420HP variants. The engine is paired with the HW19710 10-speed manual transmission and heavy-duty rear axles with a load capacity of 16 tons per axle. The robust chassis frame is constructed from high-strength steel with a section dimension of 300mm x 80mm x 8mm.

In the Philippine market, the HOWO 10 wheeler dump truck has gained significant popularity due to its competitive pricing compared to Japanese and European alternatives. The total cost of ownership is further reduced by excellent fuel efficiency and the availability of affordable spare parts through SINOTRUK's established dealer network in the Philippines.

The 10 wheeler configuration is ideal for large-scale construction projects, quarry operations, and infrastructure development. Its ability to navigate challenging terrain while carrying substantial loads makes it the preferred choice for Philippine contractors and mining companies.
